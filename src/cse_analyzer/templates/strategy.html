The strategy in one minute

Swing trading: buy pullbacks in uptrends, cut losers fast, let winners run
1 · Market

Trade with the tide

New buys only while the ASPI is above its 200-day average. Today: {% if regime.known %}{{ 'uptrend' if regime.ok else 'downtrend' }} ({{ regime.aspi|money(0) }} vs {{ regime.ma200|money(0) }}){% else %}not enough index history{% endif %}.

2 · Filter

Liquid stocks in uptrends

Skip stocks trading under LKR {{ s.min_turnover_lkr|compact }}/day, in a long downtrend, or scoring {{ '%+d'|format(avoid_max) }} or worse.

3 · Entry

Buy the pullback

Close above the 50-day, RSI back in {{ s.enter_rsi_low|money(0) }}–{{ s.enter_rsi_high|money(0) }}, volume at or above normal.

4 · Size

Risk a fixed slice

Stop {{ s.stop_loss_pct|money(0) }}% below entry (that distance is 1R). Size so a stop-out loses {{ s.risk_per_trade_pct|money(1) }}% of capital, max {{ s.max_weight_pct|money(0) }}% in one stock.

5 · Exit

Ratchet the stop up

At +{{ s.breakeven_at_r|money(1) }}R the stop moves to break-even, then trails the highest close by {{ s.chandelier_atr_mult|money(1) }}× ATR. Take a third off at +{{ s.partial_take_at_r|money(1) }}R. It never moves down.
