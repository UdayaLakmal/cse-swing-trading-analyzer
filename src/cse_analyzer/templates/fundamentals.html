How the valuation grade works

  • P/E and P/BV each score +1 at or below {{ '%g'|format(0.75 * 100) }}% of the market’s figure and −1 at or above 150% of it. A loss (EPS at or below zero) scores −1.
  • Dividend yield scores +1 when it is at least 1.25 times the market’s yield.
  • Total +2 or more is Cheap, +1 Leans cheap, 0 Fair, −1 Leans expensive, −2 or less Expensive. Without any figures it is Unknown.
  • Net assets per share is read from the “Net assets per share” line of the latest interim report. A figure from a report over 10 months old, or giving a P/BV outside 0.02 to 50, is shown with ⚠ and left out of the grade.
  • The grade and any ex-dividend date or major announcement (rights issue, split, trading halt, takeover offer) appear as context under the verdict. They do not change it.
